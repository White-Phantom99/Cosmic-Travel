⚗️ The Physics Behind the Journey

🕰️ Time Dilation Formula

t' = t / √(1 - v²/c²)

Where t' is dilated time, t is proper time, v is velocity, c is speed of light

📏 Length Contraction

L' = L × √(1 - v²/c²)

Distances appear contracted in the direction of motion

⚡ Relativistic Energy

E = γmc² where γ = 1/√(1 - v²/c²)

Total energy increases dramatically as velocity approaches c

🏃‍♂️ Relativistic Momentum

p = γmv

Momentum also increases with the Lorentz factor

🌟 Key Physics Insights

🚫 The Light Speed Barrier

As velocity approaches c, mass approaches infinity, making acceleration impossible

⏰ Time Dilation

At 99% light speed, 1 year for traveler = 7 years on Earth

🔋 Energy Requirements

Kinetic energy approaches infinity as v → c
